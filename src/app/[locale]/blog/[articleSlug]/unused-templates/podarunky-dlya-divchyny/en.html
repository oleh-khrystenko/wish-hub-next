What is a wish collection and why do you need it?

A wish collection is a list of things you want to receive. With such a list, your friends and loved ones will know exactly what you need. This helps avoid awkward situations, like receiving something you didn’t expect or two identical gifts. Wish Hub makes creating and managing collections simple and enjoyable.

How to Start Using Wish Hub

Wish Hub is a platform that helps you easily organize your wishes. To get started, you just need to follow a few simple steps.

Registration

Registering Registering is very easy: enter your contact information, create a password, and within minutes you can start using the service. After registration, you’ll have access to all the features: creating collections, adding descriptions, and attaching links.

Creating Your First Collection

After registration, you can immediately start creating collections. For example, you can create a collection for birthday wishes or items you want to receive for the holidays. For each wish, you can specify:

  • A title.

  • Add a picture.

  • A link to the product in an online store.

  • A budget to help your friends choose more easily.

  • A description explaining why you want it.

It’s very simple and convenient — just a few clicks, and you’re done.

How to Share a Collection?

When your collection is ready, it’s easy to share it with friends. Wish Hub allows you to send a link through messengers, social networks, or email. Your friends will be able to view the list, choose a wish, and even reserve it.

What is Wish Reservation?

One of the best features of Wish Hub is the ability to reserve wishes. Here’s how it works: when someone decides to fulfill a wish from your collection, they reserve it. Others will no longer be able to choose the same wish, ensuring you don’t receive duplicate gifts.

How Does It Work?

  1. A person selects a wish from your collection.

  2. Clicks the "I will fulfill wish" button.

  3. The wish is reserved for them.

It’s convenient and eliminates misunderstandings.
